# ⛵ Celtic Shodō Wind Odyssey — *Wind Waker* Archipelago, Celestial Brushes & Irish–Japanese Folk Studio

**An interactive, zero-dependency archipelago sailing and Celestial Brush puzzle game inspired by *The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker* and *Ōkami*—uniting fluid Japanese calligraphy (*Shodō* / 書道) and early Irish *Ogham* / Neolithic spiral carving with a procedural WebAudio synthesizer blending Irish folk music and Japanese traditional scales.**

---

## 🌊 Core Gameplay & 🏝️ Island Shrines

1. **Sailing & Calligraphy Wake:** Steering your boat leaves a velocity-tapered ink-and-foam wake across a cel-shaded sea chart.
2. **Celestial Brush Glyphs:** Switch to **Celestial Brush Mode** at island shrines to draw geometric gestures (Closed Circle, Directional Slash, S-Curve Wave, and Cross/Star Spark) that alter wind, tides, and beacons.
3. **Four Shrines:** 
   * *Isle of Brú & Enso* (Zen Ensō & Newgrange spirals)
   * *Ogham & Kaze Ridge* (Medieval Ogham notches & *yoko-kaku* strokes)
   * *Sean-Nós & Kana Shoals* (Irish ornamented vocals & Heian *Hiragana* poetry)
   * *Solstice & Tōrō Beacon* (Winter solstice light & stone *tōrō* lanterns)

---

## 🎶 The `D – E – G – A – B` Irish–Japanese Folk Musical Bridge

Both **Irish traditional music** (D-Major / D-Dorian pentatonic) and the **Japanese *Yo* scale** (*Yo-senpō*) share the pentatonic backbone **`D – E – G – A – B`** (`293.66` to `493.88 Hz`). This allows the mute-by-default WebAudio synthesizer to weave Irish whistles and harps with Japanese *Shakuhachi* and *Koto* into a multi-part folk ensemble.
